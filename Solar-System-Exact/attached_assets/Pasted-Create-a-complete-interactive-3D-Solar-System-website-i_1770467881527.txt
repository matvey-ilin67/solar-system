Magnetic field visualization feature (very important)

Add a button inside each planet’s information panel labeled:

"Show Magnetic Field"

Behavior must be exactly this:

• When pressed, display a visible 3D magnetic field around the planet
• Use curved field lines emerging from the north pole and entering the south pole
• Lines must form a dipole field shape
• Field rotates together with the planet
• Semi-transparent glowing lines or particles
• Scaled to realistic magnetosphere size
• Should look similar to NASA scientific visualizations

Also add:

• Toggle ON and OFF
• Only visible for planets that actually have magnetic fields (Earth, Jupiter, Saturn, etc.)
• Disabled or hidden for planets without one
